### 2026-08-06: Rework welcome/confirmation-code emails to not depend on a DB-backed `EmailTemplate` (Option B, not rebuilding the model)

Decision:
`send_welcome_email`/`generate_welcome_email`/`send_confirmation_code_email`/`generate_confirmation_code_email` (`scuba/accounts/models.py`) now generate email subject/content directly in code, instead of loading it from an `EmailTemplate` DB row.
